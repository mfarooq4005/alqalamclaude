Academic Programs

What We Teach — and How

STREAM, Robotics, Digital Classrooms, and Co-Curricular activities — all designed around one goal: building capable, thinking human beings.

STREAM Curriculum

Six Disciplines. One System.

At AL Qalam, Science, Technology, Religion, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics are not separate silos. They are taught as interconnected areas of human knowledge — just as they exist in the real world.

Science

Physics, Chemistry, Biology — Applied

Science at AL Qalam starts with a question, not a textbook. Students observe, hypothesise, experiment, and conclude. Lab time is not supplementary — it is central to every science unit.

Digital Infrastructure

A Genuinely Digital Campus

Not just projectors and PDFs. Our digital classrooms are fully interactive — students use devices to learn, create, and collaborate.

📱

Smart Boards in Every Classroom

Interactive touchscreen smart boards allow students to annotate, solve problems live on-screen, and engage with multimedia lessons that go far beyond a printed page.

💻

1:1 Device Program (Grade 4+)

Every student from Grade 4 upwards has a personal device for schoolwork — not for entertainment. Devices are tools for creation: coding, writing, designing, and researching.

📊

Student & Parent Dashboard

Real-time attendance, exam results, fee status, homework, and teacher messages — all accessible through the AL Qalam Parent App. Transparency as a default.

🎒

Digital Homework & Assignments

Assignments are submitted and returned digitally. Teachers provide detailed feedback. Students can review, resubmit, and track their own progress over time.
