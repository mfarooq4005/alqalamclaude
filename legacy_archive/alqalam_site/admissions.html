Admissions 2025–26

We welcome families who share our belief that real education is about understanding — not memorising. Here's everything you need to know about joining our school.

How to Apply

5 Simple Steps

The entire process from inquiry to enrolment takes about 2–3 weeks.

1

Submit Inquiry

Fill in the online inquiry form or visit our admissions office. Tell us which grade your child is applying for and their current academic level.

Day 1
2

Assessment Test

A short, concept-based assessment — not a rote test. We want to understand how your child thinks, not what they've memorised.

Day 3–5
3

Parent Interview

A 20-minute conversation with our Principal or Admissions Head. We want to understand your family's goals and what you expect from school.

Day 5–7
4

Offer Letter

If accepted, you receive a formal offer letter within 48 hours of the interview. The offer is valid for 7 days.

Day 7–9
5

Enrolment & Fee

Submit required documents, pay the registration and first-month fee, and your child is officially a student at AL Qalam.

Day 9–14
Who Can Apply

Age & Grade Eligibility

We admit students from Nursery through Grade 10. Age requirements follow Punjab government guidelines.

Required Documents
  • Child's original B-Form (NADRA)
  • Previous school leaving certificate
  • Last two report cards / marksheets
  • 2 recent passport-size photographs
  • Parent / Guardian CNIC (both)
  • Vaccination card (for Nursery–Grade 2)
