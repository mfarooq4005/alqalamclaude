About Us

Excellence in Education Since 2009

AL Qalam International Cambridge School has been a beacon of academic excellence in Karachi, nurturing young minds with Cambridge curriculum and Islamic values.

Cambridge Certified
Fully affiliated with Cambridge Assessment International Education. O & A Level programs with world-class resources.
Digital Smart Classrooms
State-of-the-art interactive boards, computer labs, and AI-assisted learning tools for every student.
Holistic Development
Sports, arts, drama, debating, and community service — we believe in complete personality development.
